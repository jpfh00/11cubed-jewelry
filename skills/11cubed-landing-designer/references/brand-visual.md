## Brand fit for web

| Principle | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| Exotic + exclusive | Unusual layouts, rare typography, editorial imagery — not theme-store jewelry |
| Material truth | Gold, diamonds, craft — show, don't hype with fake claims |
| Scarcity is visual | Space, slowness, restraint — not clutter |
| Foreign in a good way | Distinctive without costume or cultural pastiche |

---

## Tone on screen

- Confident, quiet, intentional
- No exclamation spam, no "BUY NOW"
- CTAs: *Request viewing*, *Private inquiry*, *Join the list* — until channel confirmed

---

## Color direction (working — refine with design system)

Until tokens are locked in `findings.md`:

- **Base:** deep black, warm charcoal, or oxidized metal dark
- **Accent:** one — antique gold, champagne, or blood-adjacent ruby — not rainbow
- **Avoid:** bright ecommerce blue, neon pink, discount red

---

## Typography direction (working)

- **Display:** high-character serif or refined geometric with edge — not system UI
- **Body:** readable, slightly warmer than tech sans
- **Avoid:** Inter, Roboto, Arial as brand voice

---

## Imagery

- Macro material texture + full-piece editorial
- No cheesy stock (smiling model + generic pendant)
- Hands/skin only with intentional art direction
