## Non-negotiables (from founder)

1. **11³ is a jewelry brand** — not a tech project first.
2. **Exotic + exclusive** — avoid generic luxury tropes.
3. **Planning mode** — strategy and branding yes; implementation no (unless explicitly unlocked).

---

## Voice & tone (working)

- Rare, confident, intentional — never desperate or discount-driven.
- Speaks to someone who values **identity and rarity**, not just carats.
- Visual and verbal language should feel **foreign in a good way** — exotic, not costume.

---

## Competitive space (mental map)

**Adjacent (study, don't copy):** high jewelry houses, independent avant-garde jewelers, exclusive DTC fine jewelry, drop-based luxury accessories.

**Not 11³:** mall chains, fast fashion jewelry, mass lab-diamond price wars, influencer "dupes" culture.
